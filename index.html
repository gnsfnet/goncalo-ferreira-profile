Bank Projects — Rating & Credit Risk Models
End-to-End Enhancement of the Corporate PD Rating System+
Context

As a Corporate Senior Credit Risk Analyst and later Credit Risk Manager at Santander, I was responsible for producing, reviewing and challenging internal ratings for a €4B corporate portfolio. The PD model was IRB aligned and used both quantitative and qualitative factors.

Problem

Over time, I observed inconsistencies in how analysts applied qualitative factors, leading to rating dispersion, unnecessary overrides and reduced stability in PD outputs — indicating weaknesses in the model's practical implementation, data inputs, and governance.

What I did (end-to-end scope)
  • Data & input review: analysed financial statements, ratio calculations, and qualitative inputs to identify inconsistencies and data quality issues.
  • Methodology assessment: evaluated the weighting of risk drivers, segmentation logic, and the alignment of qualitative factors with observed default behaviour.
  • Implementation checks: verified that the rating tool applied the methodology correctly and consistently across analysts and business units.
  • Performance analysis: reviewed discrimination, calibration, and stability metrics; assessed rating migrations and override patterns.
  • Governance & documentation: updated process documentation, introduced version control, and strengthened the audit trail.
  • Training & calibration: conducted calibration workshops with analysts to harmonise judgement and reduce subjectivity.
  • Monitoring framework: designed monthly indicators to track unusual rating movements, data anomalies, and grade distributions.
Impact
Significantly improved rating consistency, reduced overrides, enhanced PD stability, and increased senior management's confidence in the model — while strengthening governance and reducing operational risk.
Upstream Data Lineage and Input Validation for PD Model Stability+
Context

The PD model relied heavily on financial ratios automatically fed from upstream systems into the rating tool.

Problem

Several stable corporate clients experienced sudden rating deterioration without economic justification — suggesting a data lineage or transformation issue rather than a true change in creditworthiness.

What I did (end-to-end scope)
  • Source system analysis: traced financial data from the accounting system through ETL processes into the rating tool.
  • Transformation review: identified an undocumented change in the calculation of key financial ratios introduced by an upstream system update.
  • Impact assessment: quantified the effect on PD outputs, rating migrations and overrides.
  • Correction & implementation: worked with IT and Data teams to restore the correct transformation logic.
  • Control framework: introduced automated reconciliation checks and alerts for sudden ratio shifts.
  • Validation: re-tested the PD model's performance after corrections to ensure stability and alignment with expected behaviour.
Impact
Restored PD accuracy, prevented further rating distortions, and established a robust control environment that reduced operational and model risk.

Bank Projects — Scoring Models
Mortgage Affordability and Risk Scoring Model+
Context

Despite my career being made in corporate business, I was invited to take part in scoring projects for mortgage loans and consumer finance. The bank needed a structured scoring mechanism to support mortgage underwriting, combining affordability, credit history and behavioural indicators.

Problem

Mortgage decisions relied heavily on manual judgement. Affordability assessments were inconsistent, and early-warning indicators were not integrated into the underwriting process.

My role (end-to-end scope)
  • Business requirements definition: helped define the scoring model's purpose (affordability validation, risk differentiation, underwriting support) and identified key variables — debt-to-income ratio, loan-to-value, employment stability, savings behaviour, past arrears, account conduct.
  • Data feasibility & quality review: assessed availability and reliability of income, employment and collateral data; flagged inconsistencies in property valuation feeds and income verification.
  • Methodology review: challenged weighting of affordability vs behavioural indicators; ensured the model reflected real-world mortgage performance patterns.
  • Pilot testing: tested the score on historical mortgage cohorts and identified segments where it under-predicted risk.
  • Calibration review: reviewed proposed cut-offs for approval, manual review and decline; recommended adjustments for high LTV and variable-income borrowers.
  • Governance & documentation: ensured documentation met regulatory expectations (Basel, IFRS 9) and supported preparation for internal audit and model risk review.
Impact
Improved consistency in mortgage underwriting, strengthened affordability assessment and reduced approval of borderline-risk cases.
